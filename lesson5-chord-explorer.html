🎹 Music Concept: Chords & Harmony

A chord is three or more notes played together. Major chords often sound bright; minor chords often sound thoughtful. Put chords in an order and you have a progression—the harmonic path of a song.

Major: root + 4 + 3Minor: root + 3 + 4Progression: chords in motion
